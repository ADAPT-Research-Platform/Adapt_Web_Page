2. Purpose and Legal Basis for Data Processing

This policy applies to the ADAPT web application of Philipps University Marburg and the personal data collected within it.

The purpose is to collect and analyze data for scientific research as part of a study under § 3 para. 1 Hessian Higher Education Act. We assess whether the app helps reduce anxiety transmission from parents to children.

Data processing is based on your consent (Art. 6 para.1 a GDPR). Participation is voluntary and can be withdrawn at any time by emailing adapt-studie@uni-marburg.de. Withdrawal applies to future processing only; prior processing remains valid.

3. Data Being Processed

  • Identification Data: Username and password for account access.
  • Device Information: Operating system (iOS/Android) and IP address.
  • Behavioral Data:
    • Login history with timestamps and IP.
    • Time spent in the app.
    • Progress percentage.
    • Feedback entries and comments.
  • Preferences: Language and display settings.

4. Data Recipients & Retention

Data remains within Philipps University Marburg for the study duration and scientific evaluation.

Retention periods:

  • Identification: Up to 3 years, deleted within 30 days after account removal.
  • Device Data: IP deleted after 3 years; OS stored for 10 years.
  • Behavioral Data: Stored for at least 10 years for research.

5. Scientific Evaluation

Data is anonymized and aggregated for statistical analysis to evaluate app effectiveness and support research.

6. Data Subject Rights

  • Right to information

  • Right to restrict processing

  • Right to object
  • Right to lodge a complaint (datenschutz@uni-marburg.de or poststelle@datenschutz.hessen.de)

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting Philipps University Marburg. Withdrawal affects future processing only.
